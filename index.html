Before joining Cornell, I completed my B.A. in Psychological and Brain Sciences at Johns Hopkins University, with a minor in Applied Mathematics and Statistics.

My research interests include close relationships, media and gaming psychology, adolescent development, and stereotypes. I am passionate about thinking, creating, and exploring, and I hope to use psychological research to understand people’s lives with more nuance and care.

Beyond academics, I enjoy motorcycle riding, travel, photography, movies, music, video games, and anything fun and adventurous.
